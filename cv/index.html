How to use it (2 minutes)

  1. Add your CV: upload your PDF or Word file, or paste the text. Check the boxes it fills in and fix anything that looks off.
  2. Pick a job from the latest jobs drop, or paste any job ad you found.
  3. Review the match: see which skills the employer wants that your CV already shows, and tick any missing ones you truly have.
  4. Download your tailored CV as PDF. Click anywhere on the preview to edit before you download. Then apply exactly how the job says.

🔒 Your CV stays in this browser on your phone or laptop. It's only stored online if you switch on "Save my CV to my account". Your account details are kept private by Coach AY and never shared with other students. See the privacy notice at the bottom of the page.
